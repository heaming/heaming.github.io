English

Deleting your account removes every photo you've uploaded to the cloud and every album and entry on this device. This cannot be undone.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account near the bottom.
  3. Follow the prompt, type the confirmation phrase, and complete the deletion.

Request by email

If you can't install the app or prefer another way, contact us at the address below. We'll verify your account and process the request.
